Overview

Breathe new life into your phone: latest Android via custom ROM, debloated system, and optional root with Magisk / KernelSU / APatch — all flashed remotely over AnyDesk.

Popular picks: LineageOS, PixelOS, crDroid, Evolution X. Tell us your model and priorities (battery, gaming, Pixel features) and we recommend the most stable build.

Requirements

  • Unlocked bootloader — still locked? Bundle with our Xiaomi Instant Bootloader Unlock service first.
  • Full backup — internal storage is wiped.
  • Windows PC + USB cable + AnyDesk.

Warnings

  • Root and custom ROMs can break banking apps / Play Integrity — we configure DenyList / PIF where possible and explain the trade-offs honestly.
  • Camera quality may differ from stock on some models; OTAs become manual.
  • Owner-verified devices only.
